# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- New "Play ending" section: play any of the 5 endings instantly (with confirmation, since every ending wipes the save).

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- Adjustable heads chance (0–100 %) and flip duration (0.1–10 s).
- In-game menu (F1) and config file.
- Optional "Prevent ending" to stop a 10-heads streak from wiping the save.
